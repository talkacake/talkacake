🛠️ 4. Grammar Corner: Rules & Warnings

When someone is doing something crazy (like dancing in traffic), we use these words to tell them to stop:

"You shouldn't + verb" (คุณไม่ควร...)
"You must not + verb" (คุณต้องไม่... / ห้ามเด็ดขาด)

  • You shouldn't dance on the highway. It's dangerous!
  • You must not drink alcohol and drive.

Example: "You shouldn't sleep during the audit meeting!"

📱 6. Viral TikTok Games (Brain Break!)

🧠 Game 1: 8 Words in 15 Clues

How to play: The teacher gives Student A a secret category (e.g., Office Items). Student A can only say ONE word as a clue. The other students must guess the correct word. Can the team guess 8 words before Student A uses 15 clues?

🔢 Game 2: Guess The Secret Number

How to play: One student thinks of a number between 1 and 100 (or a specific real-life number). The others must ask funny questions to guess it!
Example Questions:
- Is this the number of cups of coffee you drink in a week?
- Is this the number of your ex-boyfriends?
- If I speak English in front of this many people, will I cry?

🅰️ Game 3: Two Letters (Start & End)

How to play: Two students shout out a letter at the exact same time (e.g., Student A says "M", Student B says "D"). Everyone races to think of a word that starts with M and ends with D! (Example: Mad, Mind, Mud).
