🛠️ 4. Grammar Corner: Gentle Warnings

When someone is doing something wrong on a video call, we want to be polite. We use:

"You might want to..." (คุณอาจจะอยาก... / คุณน่าจะ...)

  • You might want to turn off your microphone.
    Example situation: Your dog is barking loudly!
  • You might want to put on some pants.
    Example situation: You stood up and we can see your underwear!
  • You might want to wake up.
    Example situation: The boss is looking at you!

Your Turn! Make a funny warning sentence for your partner.

📱 7. Viral TikTok Games (Party Time!)

🅰️ Game 1: 2 Letters (Start & End)

How to play: Two students say a letter at the same time. Everyone must race to say a word that starts with the first letter and ends with the second letter.

Example: Student A says "C". Student B says "T".

Example answers: Cat, Coat, Carrot.

🔗 Game 2: The Name Chain

Category: Famous People & Politicians

How to play: Say a famous name. The next person must say a name that starts with the last letter of the previous name.

Example: Barack Obama ➡️ Angelina Jolie ➡️ Elon Musk.

🛑 Game 3: Don't Say The Same Word!

How to play: The teacher gives a category. On the count of 3, everyone says a word in that category. If two people say the same word, you lose!

Categories to try: Colors, Animals in a zoo, Office supplies, Thai food.

🔢 Game 4: Crack The Code

How to play: Each student thinks of a secret 4-digit number.

Example: 5-2-9-1.

Take turns guessing each other's numbers.

The person with the secret number can only say:

✅ "Correct number, correct place."
🟨 "Correct number, wrong place."
❌ "Wrong number."
