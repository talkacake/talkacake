Today's 7 Words

คำศัพท์วันนี้
👀
Press 🔊. Say each word 3 times. Read the 💡 trick. It helps you remember.
Нажми 🔊. Скажи каждое слово 3 раза. Прочитай 💡 подсказку. Она помогает запомнить.
👉
an níi
อันนี้
this one · 💡 Say 'an knee' and point at it. When you forget a word, an níi works for anything!
❓
à-rai
อะไร
what · 💡 In Thai, 'what' goes at the END: an níi à-rai = this one, what?
🤔
an níi à-rai
อันนี้อะไร
What's this? · 💡 this one (an níi) + what (à-rai).
💰
an níi gìi bàat
อันนี้กี่บาท
How much is this? · 💡 this one (an níi) + how many baht (gìi bàat). You already know gìi bàat!
🍜
maa-mâa
มาม่า
instant noodles · 💡 Like 'mama'. It's a famous brand, so Thai people call all instant noodles maa-mâa.
🧋
chaa nom kài-múk
ชานมไข่มุก
bubble tea · 💡 chaa (tea, like chaa kǐao) + nom (milk) + kài-múk (pearls). kài = egg: the pearls look like tiny eggs!
🔴
jùt
จุด
point; dot · 💡 The dot in a number: 7.5 = jèt jùt hâa (seven point five).
