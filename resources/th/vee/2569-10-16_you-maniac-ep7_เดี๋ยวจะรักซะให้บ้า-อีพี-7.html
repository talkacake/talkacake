We use the structure '...แล้ว...' (...láew...) to show the sequence of events. It means 'after... then...' or 'if... then...'.
Structure: (Clause 1) + láew + (Clause 2) / 'When/After X, then Y.'
Examples:

2. After we get off work, let's go eat fried rice.
3. After class is finished, I will go to the cinema.
