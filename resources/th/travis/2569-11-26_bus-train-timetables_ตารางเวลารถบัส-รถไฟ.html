Dialogue

nák-tâawng-tîeow: kɔ̌ɔ-tôot kâ. kɔ̌ɔ duu dtaa-raang way-laa rót-bát bpai hâang nɔ̀i dâi mái ká

🤔

nák-tâawng-tîeow: kɔ̀ɔp-kun kâ. rót-bát kan dtɔ̀ɔ-bpai gìi-mohng ká

Speaking Practice
🗓️
กิจกรรมที่ 1: ดูตารางเวลาแล้วตอบคำถามเค้กค่ะ
gìt-jà-gam tîi nùeng: duu dtaa-raang way-laa láew dtɔ̀ɔp kam-tăam kâke kâ
Activity 1: Look at the timetable and answer Cake's questions. (Teacher shows a simple timetable with destinations and times in Arabic numerals. E.g., To Market: 9:00, 11:30, 14:00. To Police Station: 10:15, 12:45, 15:30). Cake will ask: 'รถไปตลาดเที่ยวต่อไปกี่โมงคะ' (What time is the next trip to the market?), 'รถไปสถานีตำรวจคันสุดท้ายกี่โมงคะ' (What time is the last bus to the police station?).
👮‍♂️
กิจกรรมที่ 2: Role-play! คุณอยากไปสถานีตำรวจ
gìt-jà-gam tîi sɔ̌ɔng: Role-play! kun yàak bpai sà-tăa-nii dtam-rùat
Activity 2: Role-play! You want to go to the police station. Ask me (the staff) for the bus times. You need to ask: What time is the next bus? and What time is the last bus? Then we will swap roles.
