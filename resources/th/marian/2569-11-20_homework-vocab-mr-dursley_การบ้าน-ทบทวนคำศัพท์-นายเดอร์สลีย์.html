How to Do Your Homework

📝

1. Press 🔊 to listen first. 2. Read aloud. 3. Answer the questions. 4. For the 2 tasks with 🎙️, record your voice. Listen to it, and record again if you want to. 5. Press 📤 to send your homework to Cake.
