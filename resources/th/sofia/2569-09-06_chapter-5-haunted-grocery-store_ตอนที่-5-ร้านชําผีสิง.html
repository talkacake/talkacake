This story is about three professors: Maewnam, Chompoo, and Tonmai. They were very hungry and went to a grill restaurant. But a problem occurred, so they had to walk back to the university in the hot weather. Along the way, Tonmai told a rumor about a haunted grocery store whose old owner had just passed away, which scared everyone. But when they arrived, they found the store had become a new one named 'Chamlae' (Dismember), and the new owner was secretly watching them from inside.
