คำถามจากบทสนทนา

Questions from the Dialogue
❓
A โทรหาใครคะ
kon toh-rá-sàp (A) toh hăa krai ká
Who is the caller (A) calling for? (Expected answer: คุณจอห์น / kun-jon)
❓
คุณจอห์นรับโทรศัพท์ไหมคะ ทำไมคะ
kun-jon ráp toh-rá-sàp măi ká. tam-mai ká
Does John answer the phone? Why? (Expected answer: ไม่ได้รับ เพราะเขาไม่อยู่ / mâi dâi ráp · pró kăo mâi yòo / No, because he's not in.)
❓
A ฝากข้อความว่าอะไรคะ
kon toh-rá-sàp (A) fàak kâw-kwaam wâa à-rai ká
What message did the caller (A) leave? (Expected answer: บอกว่าเพื่อนโทรมา และให้โทรกลับ / bòk wâa pêuan toh maa láe hâi toh glàp / To say his friend called and to call him back.)

bòt-bàat sŏm-mút sŏng: long sà-thăa-ná-gaan mài ná ká. kun toh hăa kék pêua kŏr pôot gàp pêuan kŏng kék tîi chêu 'maa-riia' dtàe maa-riia mâi yòo. kun dtông fàak kâw-kwaam hâi maa-riia toh glàp
Role-play 2: Let's try a new situation.
You call Cake to ask to speak with her friend, 'Maria'. But Maria is not in. You need to leave a message for Maria to call you back.
